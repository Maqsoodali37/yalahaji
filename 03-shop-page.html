Shopping for Hajj & Umrah in Pakistan

Getting ready for pilgrimage means gathering a long list of small things, and it is easy to forget half of them until you are already in Makkah. Every kit here is built from what returning pilgrims told us they actually reached for — not a generic packing list copied off the internet.

Choosing between Economy, Standard and Premium

The tier sits on the product, not just the kit, so you can mix them freely. Economy covers the essentials at the lowest workable price. Standard upgrades fabric weight and stitching. Premium uses the softest terry cotton and knitted khaddar, with reinforced seams that survive repeated washing in Saudi heat.

What is safe to wear during Ihram

Anything scented is off-limits while in Ihram, which is why every fragrance in this catalogue is alcohol-free and every soap, shampoo and lotion in our kits is unscented. Men need two unstitched sheets; women wear ordinary modest clothing with the face and hands uncovered.

How long does delivery take across Pakistan?
Karachi, Lahore and Islamabad usually receive orders within 1–2 working days. Everywhere else in Pakistan takes 3–5 working days. Orders above ₨5,000 ship free.
Can I open the parcel before paying?
Yes. Write "Allow to open" beside your name at checkout and the courier will let you inspect the parcel before you hand over payment.
What is included in a complete Umrah kit?
Ihram set or abaya depending on selection, travel bag, unscented soap, tasbeeh, money pouch, slippers, prayer mat, miswak and a printed checklist. Premium kits add an ihram belt and a hygiene pack.
How do I choose the right abaya size?
Every abaya listing has a size chart with bust, sleeve and full-length measurements in inches. If you are between sizes, take the larger one — abayas are meant to fall loose.
Can I return something if it doesn't fit?
Return anything unused within 7 days, no questions asked. Message us on WhatsApp and we will arrange the pickup ourselves.
